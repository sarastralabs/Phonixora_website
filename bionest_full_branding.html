Our Promise

Nature's package.
Your promise.

BioNest manufactures biodegradable plastic pouches and poly bags from plant-derived biopolymers. Every product is certified compostable — returning to soil within 180 days, leaving zero microplastics behind. We believe sustainable packaging should be as strong and reliable as conventional plastic.

🌿
Plant-derived
Made from PHA and PLA — sugarcane, corn starch, and cellulose.
♻
Circular design
Closes the loop — compostable in home and industrial facilities.
🔬
Lab certified
EN 13432, ASTM D6400, and BIS 17088 compliant.
💪
No compromise
Tensile strength and barrier properties equal conventional LDPE.
